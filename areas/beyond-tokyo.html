Beyond Tokyo

Shrines visited beyond Tokyo. They will move to their own regional pages in time.

Ise

2 shrines
  • JinguIse Jingu伊勢神宮Ise, MieJingu · the Inner and Outer Shrines
  • NotableSarutahiko Jinja猿田彦神社Ise, MieNotable · the god who opens the way

Ise Jingu, which rebuilds its sanctuary every twenty years, and Sarutahiko Jinja, which enshrines the god who opens the way.

  • Ise JinguThe Inner and Outer Shrines. Shikinen Sengu, the rebuilding of the sanctuary every twenty years, has continued since the Asuka period.
  • Sarutahiko JinjaThe god who opens the way. The Uji-Doko family, said to be his descendants, have been chief priests for generations. Some visit before starting a business, changing jobs or moving house.

Hida · Takayama

2 shrines
  • Ichinomiya (1st)Minashi Jinja水無神社Takayama, GifuIchinomiya · Hida Province
  • NotableSakurayama Hachimangu桜山八幡宮Takayama, GifuNotable · the autumn Takayama Festival

The ichinomiya of Hida Province and the shrine of the autumn Takayama Festival, which divide the town of Takayama between them.

  • Minashi JinjaThe ichinomiya of Hida Province. The principal deity is Mitoshi no Okami. The mountain behind, Kuraiyama, is its sacred object.
  • Sakurayama HachimanguThe guardian shrine of the northern half of Takayama. At the autumn Takayama Festival (the Hachiman Festival), eleven floats are paraded together.

Mount Fuji

3 shrines
  • TaishaFujisan Hongu Sengen Taisha富士山本宮浅間大社Fujinomiya, ShizuokaTaisha · head of the Sengen shrines
  • NotableYamamiya Sengen Jinja山宮浅間神社Fujinomiya, ShizuokaNotable · the original shrine
  • NotableKitaguchi Hongu Fuji Sengen Jinja北口本宮冨士浅間神社Fujiyoshida, YamanashiNotable · the northern climbing route

A shrine that calms the mountain, a shrine that worships it from afar, and a shrine for climbing it: three shrines around Mount Fuji.

  • Fujisan Hongu Sengen TaishaThe head shrine of the Sengen shrines, of which more than 1,300 are said to exist across Japan. The main sanctuary, in the two-storey Sengen-zukuri style, was built under Tokugawa Ieyasu.
  • Yamamiya Sengen JinjaRegarded as the original shrine of Fujisan Hongu Sengen Taisha. It has no main hall; Mount Fuji itself is worshipped from the worship site.
  • Kitaguchi Hongu Fuji Sengen JinjaOn the northern side of Mount Fuji, at the start of the Yoshida climbing route. It grew up as a shrine for climbing the mountain.
