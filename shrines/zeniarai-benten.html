History

Origins and founding

In 1185, Minamoto no Yoritomo is said to have found the spring after a message in a dream. The shrine began with worship of that water.

Highlights

What to see

The grounds are entered through a tunnel cut into the rock / money is washed in spring water inside a cave — worshippers are said to wash the contents of their wallets.

Background

Cultural context

Benzaiten began as a goddess from India and entered Japan through Buddhism. She is a standard example of Shinto-Buddhist syncretism.
