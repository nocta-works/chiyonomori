History

Origins and founding

The shinomiya, or fourth-ranked shrine, of Sagami Province, and a shikinai-sha listed in the Engishiki. It stands in Shinomiya, Hiratsuka, a district whose name is the shrine’s own rank.

The enshrined deities are Uji no Wakiiratsuko, Oyamakui and Yamato Takeru. Oyamakui was enshrined here in the Meiji era, brought from the village’s Hie Jinja.

The torii at the entrance of the approach. The plaque reads “Sakitori Jinja”.

Highlights

What to see

The enshrined deity Uji no Wakiiratsuko is regarded as a god of learning, and people come to pray for success in entrance exams. A komainu stands beside the torii at the entrance of the approach, and a large pine rises in the grounds.

Background

Cultural context

The ranks of the shrines survive in the place names. Kawawa Jinja, the second, stands in the town of Ninomiya; Hibita Jinja, the third, in Sannomiya, Isehara; Sakitori Jinja, the fourth, in Shinomiya, Hiratsuka. A ranking of shrines from over a thousand years ago is still written into today’s addresses.
