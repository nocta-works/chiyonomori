History

Origins and founding

Minamoto no Yoriyoshi is said to have brought Hachiman from Iwashimizu Hachimangu to Yuigahama in 1063. Minamoto no Yoritomo moved the shrine to its present site in 1180. It became the centre of the Kamakura shogunate. It is a member of the National Ichinomiya Association and calls itself the ichinomiya of Sagami Province. The traditional ichinomiya is Samukawa Jinja — one example of how some provinces have more than one claimant (→ "Ichinomiya" in the article "The gods and the map's classifications").

The first torii. It stands at the Hachimangu-mae junction.

Highlights

What to see

Dankazura, the straight approach running from the sea to the shrine. It is said to form the spine of the town / the great stone steps / the Maiden stage / Genpei Ponds.

The great stone steps beyond the Maiden stage. The main shrine stands at the top.

Background

Cultural context

The warrior class and Hachiman (→ "Hachiman" in the article "The gods and the map's classifications"). The Minamoto clan adopted Hachiman as a god of war. The cult then spread across Japan with warrior governments.
